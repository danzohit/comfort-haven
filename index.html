About us

Welcome to Comfort Haven

Comfort Haven is an operating residential adult care provider based in Ikeja, Lagos. We believe everyone deserves to feel safe, valued and heard. Our approach places each person's preferences, wellbeing and independence at the centre of care.

We provide residential adult care. Contact our team to discuss individual support needs, current availability and visiting arrangements.

  • Respect for privacy, dignity and individual choices
  • Supportive relationships with residents and families
  • Attention to comfort, community and wellbeing
