About us

Welcome to Comfort Haven

Comfort Haven is an operating residential adult care provider in Ikeja, Lagos. We believe everyone deserves to feel safe, valued and heard.

Our approach centres on the individual—creating a supportive environment where dignity, independence and personal choice are respected each day.

  • Respect for privacy, dignity and individuality
  • Supportive relationships with residents and families
  • Attention to comfort, community and wellbeing
